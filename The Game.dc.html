HOW TO PLAY

Monsters and Mariners

Quoth's tiki parties have been known to draw all manner of guests, some unsavory and others... well... savory. Upon entering the party, you will receive a black envelope. Inside this envelope will be a card detailing whether you are a mariner or a monster.

If you are a monster, you will receive a sheet of red stickers. These are your bite marks. It is your goal to bite everyone at the party.

If you are a mariner, it is your duty to root out the evil from Quoth's party by accusing and condemning suspected monsters at dockhand meetings, which can be held in the council circle.

ANYONE CAN CALL A DOCKHAND MEETING...

For the mariners

Guide for Survival

- If there's a red sticker on you... you're dead.
- Friends check friends for bite marks!
- Spot a ghost that wasn't there before? Call a dockhand.
- Vote for the most suspicious.

For the monsters

Guide for the primal

- Stick a sticker, claim a victim.
- Know your monsters. They can't be killed.
- Mingle with the living and avoid suspicion.
- Be strategic about where and when to claim a victim.

What if I'm bit or accused of biting?

Congrats! You're now a ghost!

Put on your ghost sheet and enjoy the party from the ethereal world. Remember, ghosts can't quite recall how they died. Don't spoil the fun!

Ghosts may have the opportunity to provide abstract clues from beyond the veil as to whom killed them.
